# crossbow_bolt

### Skill variables

* **sk_plr_xbow_bolt_monster** - damage dealt to the monster on bolt hit.
* **sk_plr_xbow_bolt_client** - direct hit damage to the player from the non-zoomed crossbow in multiplayer. This doesn't include the explosion damage.

### Soundscripts

* **Crossbow.BoltHitBody** - hitting something that can take damage.
* **Crossbow.BoltHitWorld** - hitting something that can't take damage.
