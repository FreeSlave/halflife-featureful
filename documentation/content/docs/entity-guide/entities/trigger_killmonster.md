# trigger_killmonster

Kills monster (or multiple monsters) making them play random death animation (or gibbing depending on the option).

### Parameters

* `Monster's Name` - kill all monsters with such targetname, given they also are of specific class and squad (if set).
* `Monster's Classname` - kill all monsters with such classname, given they also are of specific name and squad (if set).
* `Monster's Squad name` - kill all monsters with such squad name, given they also are of specific name and class (if set).

### Spawnflags

* `Remove On fire` - remove this entity after it has been fired.
* `Always gib` - force gibbing.
